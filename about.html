Agricultural experience

My agricultural interests are rooted in practical production, irrigation, crop management and modern farming systems. I am especially interested in greenhouse work, soilless farming and other innovative methods that support productivity and sustainability. I previously undertook an internship at Soiless Farm Lab, where I was exposed to soilless farming, greenhouse construction and other modern agricultural practices.

Entrepreneurial journey

My agricultural entrepreneurial journey has involved setbacks and failures. These experiences have shaped my determination, improved my planning abilities, strengthened my adaptability and taught me the importance of improvisation. I see these difficulties not as defeats, but as practical lessons that are helping me develop a stronger, more realistic approach to business and problem-solving.
